# Attribution and publication scope

This is a curated publication of the RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection competition workspace, maintained by **Manya Kaistha**. The recorded competition team is **Abhi**. Official submission records identify `manyakaistha` and `zeroday19`; the original shared model release is hosted at [XERO47/rsna-knee-mri](https://github.com/XERO47/rsna-knee-mri).

The team result is shared work. Notebook ownership, a submission account or this repository's initial commit is not a claim of exclusive authorship of every component. Recovered inference programs retain their provenance in `evidence/source_manifest.json` and the version-linked submission ledger.

## Third-party inputs

- Competition data: RSNA / Kaggle, under the competition's data-access terms.
- Report-label datasets: Steven Lee Hans, dreaddevelopment and pilkwang, linked in the preparation code and reproduction guide.
- Raptor corpus: dreaddevelopment, linked in the report.
- Pretrained models and software: PyTorch, torchvision, timm, DINO/DINOv2 and their model-specific licenses and notices.

No new blanket license is applied to shared historical source or third-party model weights whose complete ownership/licensing chain was not established. Public visibility is not a replacement for a license. Original upstream terms continue to apply; contact the relevant rights holders before reuse that requires permission.

## Publication choices

This repository publishes source, aggregate evidence and artifact references. It excludes patient data, raw reports, patient-level predictions/labels, annotation databases, credentials, local environments, transient logs and development prompts. Scientific references to report-label extraction and self-supervised learning are retained because they explain the actual method.
